eumemic credits us in the README of https://github.com/eumemic/exoplanets (section "Credits",
https://github.com/eumemic/exoplanets#credits), added in their commit ba850b4 on 2026-10-19
("README: how outside contributors are credited, and who they are so far"). Copied here for the record.

What it says about us:
- Josh Gold (killercatfish) is named as an outside contributor, for issue #2: TOI-913, galaxies in the
  TIC, and the per-sector centroids.
- Their rule: outside contributors whose issues or pull requests change the pipeline or the published
  results are credited in that release's note, by name or GitHub handle, with a link to the issue or PR.
- The other two named are ahrazzle (many issues and pull requests) and Antoine Olivier (issue #1).

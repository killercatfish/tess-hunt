One more check, in case it's useful for your candidates too. The 27.3 d systematic is close to five times 5.45 d, so I tested whether 5.45 d could be its 1/5 harmonic. 5P = 27.2507 ± 0.0001 d against 27.2852 d, so the two drift apart by 3.2 d (0.59 of a 5.45 d period) over the 2,524-day baseline. Of the 34 observed transits, the 32 that fall more than 1.4 d from any 27.3 d event carry the full depth: 179 ± 22 ppm, SNR 8.2. A harmonic would put all of its depth in the transits that coincide with the systematic. Code: scripts/harmonic_systematic_test.py in https://github.com/killercatfish/tess-hunt.
